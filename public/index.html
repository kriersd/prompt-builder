Prompt Types

System

Sets persistent instructions the AI follows for every response — role, tone, rules, and boundaries. Best for configuring an assistant's overall behavior.

Task

Describes a specific one-time job: writing, coding, analysis, summarization. The AI focuses entirely on completing the defined task.

Few-Shot

Teaches the AI the desired output pattern by providing example input/output pairs. Effective when format or style consistency is critical.

Chain of Thought

Instructs the AI to reason step-by-step before giving a final answer. Improves accuracy on complex, multi-step problems and logical tasks.

ReAct

Combines reasoning and acting — the AI plans, calls tools, observes results, and iterates. Ideal for agentic workflows that interact with external systems.
